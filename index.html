Overview概览

LLM agents change what misinformation is, and give us new ways to study it大语言模型智能体改变了虚假信息的面貌，也为研究它提供了新方法

A single model can fabricate a plausible claim. An agent can refine that claim against feedback, tailor it to an audience, push it through a social network, and gradually change what people believe. The same agents also give researchers a laboratory in which the spread of falsehoods, the drift of beliefs, and the effect of corrections can be simulated, measured, and stress-tested before they play out on real platforms.单个模型就能编造出一条看似可信的说法；智能体则更进一步：它能根据反馈反复打磨这条说法，针对不同受众调整措辞，再借助社交网络扩散出去，一点点改变人们的看法。另一方面，这些智能体也为研究者提供了一个实验场：在虚假信息真正在平台上蔓延之前，就可以先对它的传播、观点的变化以及纠正措施的效果进行模拟、测量和压力测试。

This tutorial covers recent work in this area through three questions:本次 Tutorial 围绕三个问题，介绍这一方向的最新研究：

  1. How do agents generate misinformation, from unintended hallucination to goal-directed fabrication and the co-evolution of generators and detectors?智能体如何生成虚假信息？从无意中产生的幻觉，到有目的的编造，再到生成器与检测器在对抗中的共同演化。
  2. How does misinformation propagate as LLM personas spread, distort, and polarize around content, and when can such simulations be trusted to reflect real populations?虚假信息如何传播？由 LLM 扮演的用户会转发、歪曲内容，观点也会随之两极分化；这类模拟在什么情况下才能可信地反映真实人群？
  3. How can agents intervene, through evidence retrieval, multi-agent fact-checking and debate, and correction strategies, and how reliable are the verifiers themselves?智能体如何干预虚假信息？手段包括证据检索、多智能体事实核查与辩论、纠正策略等；而负责核查的智能体本身又有多可靠？

Who should attend适合人群

Researchers, students, and practitioners in NLP, information retrieval, social computing, trustworthy AI, and multi-agent systems.自然语言处理、信息检索、社会计算、可信 AI 和多智能体系统等方向的研究者、学生和从业者。

Prerequisites预备知识

Basic familiarity with machine learning, NLP, and large language models. No prior experience with agents or social simulation is assumed.具备机器学习、自然语言处理和大语言模型的基础知识即可，不要求有智能体或社会模拟方面的经验。

Outline大纲

  1. Part I第一部分 ~70 min约 70 分钟

    Misinformation Generation虚假信息的生成

    • LLM-generated misinformation: mechanisms and methodsLLM 生成虚假信息的机制与方法
    • Goal-directed misinformation generation with agents基于智能体的目标导向型虚假信息生成
    • Adversarial co-evolution of generators and detectors生成器与检测器的对抗式共同演化

    From hallucination and source distortion to agents that draft, evaluate, and revise narratives, and detectors that must keep up.从幻觉与信源歪曲，到能够起草、评估并修改叙事的智能体，以及需要随之升级的检测器。

  2. Part II第二部分 ~70 min约 70 分钟

    Agent-Based Propagation Simulation基于智能体的传播模拟

    • Modeling users, social networks, and interactions用户、社交网络与交互建模
    • Content evolution, belief dynamics, and polarization内容演变、观点动态与两极分化
    • Evaluation and validation of social simulations社会模拟的评估与验证

    How LLM personas pass content along, how it mutates, how beliefs shift, and how to check a simulation against real data.LLM 扮演的用户如何传递内容、内容如何变形、观点如何转变，以及如何用真实数据检验模拟结果。

  3. Part III第三部分 ~70 min约 70 分钟

    Agent-Based Verification and Intervention基于智能体的核查与干预

    • Evidence retrieval and multi-agent fact-checking证据检索与多智能体事实核查
    • Misinformation correction and intervention strategies虚假信息的纠正与干预策略
    • Reliability and dynamic evaluation of verification agents核查智能体的可靠性与动态评估

    Agents that retrieve evidence, debate, and write corrections, and the failure modes that make verifiers unreliable.能够检索证据、相互辩论并撰写更正内容的智能体，以及让核查者变得不可靠的各种失效模式。
